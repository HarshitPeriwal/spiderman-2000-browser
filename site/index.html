Spider-Man (2000) in the browser

Runs the original Windows PC release from your own copy, unchanged. Your files stay on your computer and are never uploaded.

0 Browser check

    1 Select your installed game folder

    Pick the folder that contains SpideyPC.exe, binkw32.dll, data.pkr and media.pkr. It's only read, never changed.

    Your browser may ask whether to "upload" the files. Nothing is sent anywhere: the files are only read by this page, on your computer.

      2 Install into this browser

      The game files are copied once into this browser's private storage (about 460 MB) and stay there, so later visits start straight away. They never leave your computer. DirectX wrapper DLLs such as dgVoodoo are left out, because they would replace the browser's graphics layer. Saved games are kept in the same storage.

      3 Play

      The game starts on the next screen. Click it once to enable sound. A controller works too: press any button after the game starts.
